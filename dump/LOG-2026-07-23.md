Why am I rambling about this. This was supposed to be a piece about creating my first video game. Well it was because of this very moment I got interested into games. 

You see, I ended up homeless that night. The true cause of the crackles and pops, and my cat's stinky hisses, was rats. Dozens of them. Lounging in my food cupboards for the past year, enjoying the pasta boxes I had stored before I left.

I hav one rule in my life. Never trust a rat. So I damn sure was not going to sleep in the same apartment as one. For exactly 47 minutes, I experienced the cold brutal reality of being homeless. But then my buddy Mitch messaged me to play some games, and after hearing I was on the streets, he gladly welcomed my Cat into his house.

Me on the other hand. I was allowed to crash on the couch, but he had excatly one rule I had to follow.

Play Video Games.

You ever play video games like your very survival depended on it? At first it kind of takes the fun out of it, but after some time, you realize this is the only time you can play guilt free.

So I played Video Games. Dozens of them. Good ones. My boy Mitch had impeccable taste.
He opened my eyes to the possibilities of the art form. Beyond the hyper realism that the mainstream industry chases every year. Good games can be found anywhere!
